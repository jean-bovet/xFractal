- Hebrew and Arabic: xFractal is now available in both languages on iPhone, iPad and Mac. With the device set to Hebrew or Arabic, the Controls, the panel and the buttons are laid out right to left, while the fractal itself, its coordinates and its numbers stay exactly as they are and are never mirrored.

- All languages: the iteration count at the bottom of the screen and the Julia constant always show Western digits (0 to 9), also when the system language uses other digits.

- No other visible changes: this build also carries the tooling behind the gallery and the screenshots.

Please verify: switch the device to Hebrew or Arabic and check that the Controls and the panel read right to left while the fractal and its numbers are unchanged; on an iPad or iPhone Duo, check the side panel opens on the correct side and the fractal follows it; in your usual language, check the iteration count still updates as you zoom.
