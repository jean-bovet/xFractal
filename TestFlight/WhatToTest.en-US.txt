- xFractal is now available in French, German and Italian, on iPhone, iPad and Mac: every label, palette and Julia preset follows your device's language.

- New app icon appearances on iPhone and iPad: a dark version for the dark Home Screen and a tinted one for tinted icons.

- Your exploration history is no longer cut short when the app starts, so Replay plays back your whole session after a relaunch.

- Mac: the parameter panel button no longer goes blank while the panel is open; it now stays highlighted until you close the panel.

Please verify: switch the device language to French, German or Italian and check the controls read naturally, try the dark and tinted Home Screen icons, and on the Mac open and close the parameter panel from its button.
