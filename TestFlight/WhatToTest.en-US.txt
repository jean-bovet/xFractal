- iPhone Duo: xFractal is now built for the Duo. On the open inner display the fractal fills the whole screen, and the Controls open as a side panel next to the fractal instead of a sheet. The panel stays open when you fold the phone to the outer display and unfold it again.

- iPhone Duo, half folded: the fractal name and iteration count (the capsule at the bottom) move off the hinge so they stay readable.

- iPad and iPhone Duo: when the Controls panel slides in or out, the fractal now narrows and widens smoothly with it instead of jumping to its new size.

- iPad: the Controls open as a side panel on the right, like on the Duo; on iPhone they stay a bottom sheet. The fractal keeps the whole screen while the panel is closed.

- Mac: no visible changes in this build.

Please verify: on an iPhone Duo or iPad, open and close the Controls and watch the fractal follow the panel smoothly; on the Duo, fold and unfold the phone with the Controls open, and half-fold it to see the capsule step off the hinge; on iPhone, check the Controls still open as a bottom sheet.
