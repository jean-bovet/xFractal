Explore the infinite beauty of fractals on your Mac. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan, and dive into the endless detail of the Mandelbrot set and its relatives — rendered in real time on your Mac's GPU.

xFractal, first released for iPhone in 2008, is back — rebuilt from the ground up for Mac, iPhone and iPad. Buy once, use it on all of them.

FEATURES:
• Mandelbrot, Julia, Newton and Multibrot fractals
• Deep zoom: magnify the Mandelbrot and Julia sets more than a trillion times
• Four classic palettes — Hot, Cold, Gray and Chromatic — with smooth coloring
• Julia presets (Dendrite, Spiral, Rabbit, Galaxy, San Marco), Multibrot exponents 2–8, three Newton polynomials
• Replay: watch your whole exploration animate back as a short movie
• Picks up exactly where you left off
• Free and open source

Deep zoom uses K. I. Martin's perturbation technique.
